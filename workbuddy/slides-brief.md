# Slide deck brief — Movents (Dormathon 2026, Track 2)

7 slides, 3-minute pitch. Ask WorkBuddy to build and export to PDF.

1. **Problem** — Societies run events across email, spreadsheets and chat. Every university has its own SOP for
   proposals, approvals, venues and claims; committees learn it by trial and error.
2. **Gap** — ClickUp, ibl.ai and Cvent give generic workflows. None follow *your* university's SOP.
3. **Movents** — "One prompt plans your event the way your university requires it, and only the right people
   can approve it." WorkBuddy is the agent; Movents holds SOP, roles, approvals, audit log, outputs.
4. **Live demo flow** — prompt → plan → research (WorkBuddy web search) → draft → validate (budget, SOP sections, lead times) →
   adapt (venue clash, over budget → cheaper vendors) → act (role-gated emails) → report. Screenshot of the trace.
5. **Trust** — role-based access, approval queue, faculty approve-by-link, audit log. Committee member is blocked;
   President approves; email sends; chain recorded.
6. **Verifiable outputs** — proposal (PDF/Word, SOP format), budget sheet with real supplier links, risk register,
   calendar feed, speaker & faculty emails, post-event report with NPS and lessons fed back into the SOP.
7. **Roadmap** — multi-university onboarding (built), Luma/Meetup live APIs (built, need keys), attendance
   prediction (built), mobile check-in PWA (built); next: inbox reply detection, sponsorship finder.
